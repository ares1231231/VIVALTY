1. What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. We also use similar technologies such as local storage for preferences (e.g. cookie consent).

2. How we use cookies

Strictly necessary

Required for the Platform to function. Cannot be disabled.

  • Session & authentication — keep you signed in securely.
  • CSRF protection — prevent cross-site request forgery on forms.
  • Cookie consent — remember your cookie preference.

Functional

Improve experience; optional where consent is required.

  • Saved filters, favourites and dashboard preferences.
  • Anonymous AI chat session continuity.

Analytics & advertising (if enabled)

We may use analytics or advertising pixels (e.g. Google Ads, TikTok Pixel) to measure campaigns and site usage. These are only activated where you have consented via our cookie banner, or where permitted without consent under applicable law.

3. Third-party cookies

Some pages load resources from trusted providers (fonts, bot protection, embedded maps). Those providers may set their own cookies subject to their policies.

4. Managing cookies

On your first visit we show a cookie banner where you can accept or decline non-essential cookies. You can also clear cookies via your browser settings. Blocking strictly necessary cookies may prevent login and forms from working.
