2. What data we collect

3. How we use your data

We process personal data to:

  • Provide and secure the Vivalty platform (accounts, listings, favourites, dashboard).
  • Respond to enquiries and connect buyers with listing partners.
  • Send service emails (verification, password reset, transactional notices).
  • Improve our scoring methodology, simulator and AI advisor.
  • Comply with legal obligations and prevent fraud or abuse.
  • Send market intelligence emails where you have opted in (you may unsubscribe at any time).

4. Legal bases (EEA & UK users)

Where GDPR applies, we rely on: contract (providing the service), legitimate interests (security, analytics, product improvement), consent (marketing emails, non-essential cookies) and legal obligation.

We do not sell your personal data. We may share data with:

  • Listing agencies when you enquire about a property.
  • Service providers that host our infrastructure, send email, store media or power AI features — under data-processing agreements.
  • Authorities when required by law.

6. International transfers

Data may be processed in the United Kingdom, European Economic Area or other countries where our providers operate. We use appropriate safeguards where required.

7. Retention
